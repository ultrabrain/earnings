4 ·Discipline — read before you click buy

  • No naked options into a known event. NFP/FOMC = elevated IV → buy a spread or wait.
  • Don't chase a bounce in a downtrend. A green day below the 50- & 200-day average is a shorting opportunity.
  • Respect the regime. When tech is rolling over, take longs in the rotation-leader sectors instead.
  • Define max loss before entry. Premium paid ≤ your max-risk-per-trade.
  • If nothing aligns, the trade is cash.
  • Invalidate fast. If the "wrong if" line triggers, you're out — no averaging down.
